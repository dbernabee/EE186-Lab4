### Part 3: DAC



###### (1 Point) If B = 9, what is Vout? Express your answer in terms of Vref . Use the DAC mode that provides the best resolution available on your MCU (refer to the data sheet).
###### 12-bit provides best resolution: Vout = (B / 2^n), Vout = (9 / 4096) * Vref = 0.002 * Vref

###### (3 Point) How does the sampling rate of the DAC affect the quality of the output waveform? What are the differences in sound perception between sine, square, and sawtooth waves? Why do we need a capacitor in the signal path when sending an audio signal to a speaker or earphones?
###### A bigger sampling rate tends to be better in quality of the output waveform compared to a smaller sampling rate. Why? Because you are taking more snapshots of the waveform, which improves the precision in which the waveform is produced. It's a lot smoother! 

###### Sine wave sounds fairly smooth, especially compared to a square wave, which sounds like a knock on the door. A sawtooth wave sounds closer to what a sine wave sounds like, but a bit more loud and harsh. 

### The capacitor is needed in the signal path so that the earbuds can read the sine wave at 0V. The earbuds require an AC signal around 0V because of the DC offset, which is generated by the DAC that is between 0 and 3.3v.
